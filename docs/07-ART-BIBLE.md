# 07 — Art Bible v0.1

## Direction

**Neo-Asian Spirit Fantasy**: modern social city + stylized East/Southeast Asian architectural motifs + spirit mythology + magical technology.

Avoid becoming:
- direct historical reconstruction;
- generic feudal-Japan anime world;
- direct Chinese-fantasy clone;
- visual imitation of a copyrighted franchise.

## World contrast

### Social state
Warm daylight/lantern warmth, market life, greenery, wood/stone, readable social silhouettes.

### Spirit invasion state
Cool violet/teal, altered sky, portal lighting, supernatural silhouettes and controlled high-contrast VFX.

The transformation must preserve recognizable city landmarks so players feel the *same place* has changed.

## Character strategy

Use one or a small number of compatible humanoid rigs with modular:
- body/face;
- hair;
- top/bottom;
- gloves/shoes;
- weapons;
- accessories.

This is a production and future cosmetic requirement.

## Hero custom assets first

Prioritize bespoke work for:
- player silhouette;
- signature boss;
- spirit companions;
- spirit gate;
- Linh Thành landmarks;
- key UI icon language.

Background environment production may use modular kits provided the final art direction is unified.

## VFX readability

Combat VFX must preserve:
- player silhouette;
- enemy telegraph readability;
- boss critical zones;
- dodge window readability.

Beauty does not override combat legibility.
