# 00 — Product Vision

## Working title

**LINH GIỚI ONLINE**

## Category

Social Action MMORPG.

## North Star fantasy

The player has a persistent second life in a vibrant modern Asian spirit world. The same city where players chat, trade, decorate homes and meet friends can transform into a cooperative battlefield when spirit gates open.

## Product promise

1. **Action:** combat is worth playing for its own sake.
2. **Social:** identity and relationships are first-class progression.
3. **Persistence:** one account becomes more meaningful over years, not seasons of full resets.
4. **Economy:** combat, gathering, crafting and trade create value for other players.
5. **Accessibility:** mobile-first controls and scalable rendering, with PC cross-play.

## Signature experience

`Social City -> warning -> Spirit Gate opens -> city changes -> cooperative world event -> boss -> contribution reward -> city restores -> players celebrate / trade / visit homes.`

## Founder Alpha scope

Founder Alpha intentionally contains only enough systems to prove the full loop:

- 1 social city;
- 2 field zones;
- 1 dungeon;
- 1 signature world event;
- 2 Paths/classes;
- about 12 active skills total plus ultimates;
- 3 spirit companions;
- 8–12 regular enemy archetypes;
- 3 elites;
- 2 bosses;
- level 1–20;
- party, friend, chat;
- guild-lite;
- inventory/equipment/loot;
- crafting-lite;
- marketplace-lite;
- one-room housing-lite;
- no PvP in Founder Alpha.

## Explicit non-goals before Founder Alpha

- 30v30 guild siege;
- seamless continent-scale open world;
- marriage;
- cross-server war;
- open-world PvP;
- 5+ launch classes;
- 15 professions;
- complex player territory system;
- custom UDP stack;
- dozens of microservices;
- pay-to-win exclusive stats.

## Product gate rule

Every large expansion must answer:

> Does this make players want to live, grow or fight together?

If the answer is weak, the feature is not automatically accepted into scope.
