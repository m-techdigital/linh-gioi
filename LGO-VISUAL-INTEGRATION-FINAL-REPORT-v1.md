Final decision: LGO_ART_V1_EXPERIMENTAL_SLICE_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Player-facing runtime copy remains code-owned and Vietnamese where touched by M6 combat UI. Reference boards, full sheets, and experimental crops are not imported into Unity runtime paths.

Non-claims: no production art, no new gameplay mechanic, no protocol/schema/ADR/design-token change.
